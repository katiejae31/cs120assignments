Greece, 2025

In September 2025, I took a two week trip to Greece with two of my best friends. We flew to the island of Crete, and explored the town of Chania and the pink sand beach, Elafonissi. Then, we took the infamous Greek ferries to Santorini and Paros, before finishing our trip in Athens. Greece had been a bucket list country of mine for a while, and it was the 27th country I've traveled to!

My Favorite Restaurant

It's not a restaurant, but one of my favorite places to get lunch in downtown Boston is Paisani, a food truck that serves up the best chicken cutlet sandwiches in the entire state of Massachusetts. The Caprese is my favorite, and features a honey whipped ricotta that's to die for. Every time the truck was parked in Dewey Square (right outside my office), it was worth the sometimes 20-minute wait in line!
Check out their website!

My Favorite Food

Every Christmas, I make cinnamon rolls from scratch. I make the dough, leave for a family dinner while it rises, then come home to add the cinnamon sugar and butter, roll them up, and cut the different rolls and finally finish around midnight. Then in the morning, I wake up and bake the rolls and whisk together a cream cheese frosting. Then, I serve them straigth out of the oven to my family!

Title Author Cover Summary
Everyone in My Family Has Killed Someone Benjamin Stevenson In this twisty whodunit... everyone's done it! It's a dark comedy centered on a family reunion at a ski resort, where, you guessed it, everyone in the family has killed someone.
A Shadow in the Ember Jennifer L. Armentrout A fantasy romance loosely based on the tale of Hades and Persephone, A Shadow in the Ember tells the tale of Sera, a forgotten princess of a dying land who must marry - and then kill - the god of Death to save her people.
The Art of Racing in the Rain Garth Stein Narrated by Enzo, this book tells the story of family, love, and loss through the eyes of the loyal dog of racecar driver Denny. Warning: only read this book if you feel like crying.
Love, Theoretically Ali Hazelwood The worlds of romance and physics combine in the third book from Ali Hazelwood. With rivals-to-lovers tension, the classic comedy you can expect from Ali Hazelwood, and way more information about the differences between theoretical and experimental physics than you'll have ever expected to know, Love Theoretically is one of my favorite books.
